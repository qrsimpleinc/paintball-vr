You start with a pistol. More weapons lie on the map (shotgun, burst rifle, scope rifle, launcher, sprayer, grenade, mine, shield, foam mallet): take one and it's yours until you drop it. One weapon per hand.
Quest: Grip near a weapon to grab it, let go to drop it where it is. Grenades: let go and grab again quickly to light one (3 seconds), then swing and let go to throw it. Grab an enemy grenade before it goes off to make it yours, or shoot one to set it off. Trigger fires (hold for the sprayer), B/Y reloads. Release a mine against a floor or wall to stick it there. Swing the mallet into an enemy. Look through the rifle scope to zoom. Left stick moves (click to run), right stick snap-turns (click to crouch, or just crouch for real), A/X jumps.
Desktop: WASD + mouse, left click = right hand, right click = left hand (fire / raise shield). Grenade: click or G lights it, again throws it; E catches an enemy grenade you're looking at. E pick up, Q drop right, V drop left, X swap hands, G throw grenade, R reload, Z zoom (scope rifle), click with a mine to place it, click with the mallet to swing, Space jump, Shift run, C crouch (hold), Tab scores, M mute.
